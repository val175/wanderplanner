## Why use a travel packing list app?

A travel packing list app makes sure you never arrive at your destination missing something important. Rather than writing notes on scraps of paper or rebuilding a list from scratch every trip, a packing list app gives you a reusable, organised checklist you can customise for any type of travel.

Stippl's packing list feature comes with pre-built templates organised by trip type — beach holiday, business trip, hiking, city break and more. Start with a template that matches your trip, remove what doesn't apply and add the specific items you need. The checklist stays with your trip so packing information and travel plans are in the same place.

For group travel, shared packing lists prevent the classic problem of everyone packing the same things and no one packing the essential ones. Invite your travel companions to the shared list and assign specific items to specific people — so everyone knows exactly what they're responsible for bringing.

Business travel has its own packing logic. Stippl's business travel packing list template covers the essentials — tech gear, travel documents, formal and casual clothing layers — so you're not rebuilding the list from scratch before every work trip. Customise it once for your typical setup, then adjust per destination. Whether you're packing for a two-day client visit or a week-long conference, the template handles the baseline.

Yes. Shared packing lists let everyone in the trip see and check off items. You can assign specific items to specific people so there's no confusion about who is bringing what.
