## What is a digital travel journal?

A digital travel journal is a way to document your trips beyond photos — capturing the details, stories and personal moments that make each destination memorable. Unlike a photo album or social post, a travel journal gives you space to write about the experience: what surprised you, what you'd do differently, the places only locals know about.

Stippl's travel journal feature is tied to your itinerary. Write entries for individual destinations, attach your photos to specific moments on the trip and keep everything organised within the trip that inspired it. Your journals build into a searchable personal travel archive you can revisit any time.

When you're ready to share, Stippl lets you publish your travel journal to the Stippl community. Other travellers can discover your entries, get inspiration for their own trips and follow along on your adventures. Your personal notes become a resource for people planning the same destinations — and a record of your travels that goes well beyond a grid of photos.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Stippl's travel journal app free?

Yes. Writing and sharing travel journals in Stippl is completely free on web, iOS and Android.

### Can I add photos to my travel journal?

Yes. Upload photos directly to your journal entries and they appear alongside your written notes. Your journal becomes a combined photo and story record of the trip.

### Can I keep my travel journal private?

Yes. Journals are private by default. You choose when to publish an entry to the Stippl community — unpublished journals are only visible to you.

### Does Stippl's travel journal work offline?

Yes. Write journal entries offline in the Stippl mobile app and they sync automatically when you're back online.

### Can I use Stippl as a travel diary app?

Yes. Stippl's travel journal feature works as a digital travel diary — write destination-by-destination entries, add photos and build a lasting record of every trip.
