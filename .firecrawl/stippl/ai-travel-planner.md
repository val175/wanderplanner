## What is an AI travel planner?

An AI travel planner generates a complete, day-by-day travel itinerary for you — based on your destination, dates, travel style and preferences. Instead of spending hours researching across multiple tabs, you describe your trip and the AI does the planning work in minutes.

Stippl's AI travel planner builds a personalised itinerary that reflects how you actually travel. Want a slow-paced food-focused trip through Italy? Or a packed adventure across Southeast Asia? The AI adapts to your pace, budget and interests — then lets you edit every detail inside Stippl's full trip planning app.

Once your itinerary is ready, it lives alongside Stippl's full set of travel tools: route maps, budget tracker, packing lists and travel journals. Your AI travel plan becomes a working document you can share with travel companions and access on any device.

AI travel planning is especially useful for complex or multi-destination trips. Planning two weeks across Japan, a road trip through Portugal or a group holiday with different preferences used to mean hours of spreadsheets and browser tabs. Stippl's AI handles the structure — destinations, day-by-day flow, what to see and where to eat — so you can focus on the parts that make a trip yours.

Unlike hiring a travel agent or using a static itinerary template, an AI trip planner responds to your inputs in real time. Change your travel style from adventurous to relaxed, swap a destination, or cut the trip short — the AI rebuilds the plan around your updated preferences instantly. It's the fastest way to go from 'I want to travel somewhere' to a complete, personalised itinerary.
