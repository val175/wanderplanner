## What is a Travel Reel?

A Travel Reel is an automatically generated video that turns your trip into a shareable 3D animation — your full route traced across a globe, paired with the photos you took along the way. No editing software, no stitching clips together, no manual work. Plan a trip in Stippl, upload your photos as you travel, and the reel generates itself.

Stippl creates Travel Reels by combining the geographic data from your itinerary with the photos from your trip journals. The result is a video that shows your exact route — flying between cities, zooming into destinations — with your photos appearing at the relevant stops. It's part travel map, part highlight reel, and it's ready to share in minutes.

Travel Reels are designed for social media. The format and aspect ratio work for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Stories. Share your trip as something people actually want to watch, not just a list of photos or a static map screenshot. The 3D globe animation gives it a visual quality that's difficult to create manually, even with dedicated video editing tools.

Every trip you plan in Stippl automatically has a Travel Reel waiting to be generated. Add photos to your journals during the trip, generate the reel when you're done and share it — the whole process takes minutes. For travellers who want to document and share their trips without spending hours in post-production, it's the fastest way to go from trip to shareable content.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the Travel Reel feature free?

Yes. Generating and sharing Travel Reels is free in Stippl. Plan a trip, add photos to your journals and generate your reel at no cost.

### How does Stippl generate a Travel Reel automatically?

Stippl combines your trip itinerary (destinations and dates) with the photos you upload to your travel journals. The reel animates your route on a 3D globe and weaves in your photos at each stop — no editing required.

### Can I share my Travel Reel on Instagram or TikTok?

Yes. Travel Reels are formatted for social media sharing. Download your reel and post it directly to Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts or any other platform.

### Do I need to add photos to get a Travel Reel?

Photos make the reel richer, but you can generate a reel from your route alone. The more photos you add to your trip journals, the more personalised the reel becomes.

### How long is a Stippl Travel Reel?

Travel Reel length depends on how many destinations and photos your trip includes. Most reels run between 30 seconds and a few minutes — long enough to tell the story, short enough to share.
