## What is a personal travel map?

A personal travel map is a digital map where you save the places that matter to you — places you've visited, restaurants you love, hidden spots you've discovered and destinations you want to explore next. Instead of scattering recommendations across notes apps, browser bookmarks and WhatsApp messages, a travel map puts everything on one interactive layer you can actually use.

Stippl's travel map is built around destinations, not just points. For every city or location, you can build a curated collection of places: where to eat, where to stay, what to see, what to skip. Add personal notes to each spot so future-you (or your friends) know exactly why it's worth visiting. Your map becomes a living travel guide shaped by your own experience.

Sharing is built into the core of the feature. Send your map to a friend planning the same destination and they get instant access to your recommendations — no copying, no screenshots, no 'I'll send you the link later'. You can also follow other travellers on Stippl and discover their maps, which turns the whole platform into a peer-sourced travel guide built on real experience rather than sponsored rankings.

Your map connects directly to your trip itineraries. Places saved on your map can be pulled into any trip you're planning, so scouting and planning happen in the same tool. Bucket-list destinations go from wishlist to itinerary in a single step.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Stippl's travel map free?

Yes. Saving places, building your personal travel map and sharing it with friends is completely free in Stippl on web, iOS and Android.

### Can I share my travel map with friends?

Yes. Share your map directly with friends so they can browse your recommendations for any destination. Your map works as a personal travel guide other people can actually use.

### Can I save a bucket list on my travel map?

Yes. Mark destinations as places you want to visit and they appear as wishlist spots on your map. Your bucket list and your visited places live side by side so you always know what's next.

### Does the travel map work with my trip itineraries?

Yes. Places saved on your map can be added directly to any trip itinerary in Stippl. Discover a great restaurant while browsing your map and drop it into your upcoming trip in one step.

### Does Stippl's travel map work on mobile?

Yes. Your personal travel map is fully accessible on iOS and Android. Browse your saved places, add new spots and share recommendations from your phone.
