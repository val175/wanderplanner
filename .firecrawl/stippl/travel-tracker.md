## What is a travel tracker app?

A travel tracker app lets you share your journey in real time with the people who care about where you are — family back home, friends joining you later, or your whole social following. Instead of sending group message updates or posting stories one by one, a travel tracker gives everyone a single live view of your route and adventures as they happen.

Stippl's travel tracker is connected to your trip itinerary. Your route is already mapped from the moment you plan the trip, and as you travel you layer real-time updates on top of it — uploading photos from each destination, sharing moments as they happen and keeping your followers in the loop without the effort of managing multiple platforms.

The shareable link is the key feature. Send one link to whoever you want to follow your journey and they get a live view of where you are and what you're experiencing — no app download required on their end. Family who aren't on Stippl can follow along just by opening the link in a browser.

Travel tracking in Stippl works alongside the rest of your trip. Journal entries you write during the trip appear on the tracker timeline. Places you visit get marked on your map. The tracker becomes a live version of your trip that everyone can follow, and a travel diary that builds itself as you go.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Stippl's travel tracker free?

Yes. Sharing your live journey and keeping followers updated is completely free in Stippl on iOS and Android.

### How does the Stippl travel tracker work?

Share a link to your trip and anyone with the link can follow your journey in real time. Upload photos on the go and your followers see your route and updates as they happen.

### Does my family need the Stippl app to follow my journey?

No. Anyone can follow your journey via the shareable link in a web browser — no app download or Stippl account required.

### Can I use Stippl to share my location while travelling?

Yes. Stippl's travel tracker lets you share your current location and route with family and friends while you travel. You control who has the link and can stop sharing at any time.

### Does the travel tracker work offline?

You can write journal entries offline in the Stippl mobile app and they sync to your tracker when you're back online. Photo uploads require a connection.
