## What is a social travel platform?

A social travel platform is a place where travellers share trips, recommendations and experiences with each other — not as polished highlights-only posts, but as real travel knowledge that's actually useful to other people planning the same places. It sits between a social network and a travel guide: the content comes from real travellers, the structure makes it searchable and the connections make it personal.

Stippl's travel community is built around the places you've been and the trips you've taken. Your profile shows your travel history, your maps and your journals. Follow friends and fellow travellers to see where they've been, what they recommend and where they're going next. The discovery is peer-sourced — you're getting recommendations from people whose taste you know, not anonymous reviews from strangers.

Sharing on Stippl isn't just posting photos. You share the full trip — the itinerary, the places you loved, the restaurants worth the queue and the ones to skip. Other travellers can browse your published trips and use them as inspiration or a starting point for their own planning. Your experiences become genuinely useful to people planning the same destinations.

The social layer connects to everything else in Stippl. Your map of favourite spots, your travel journals and your scratch map are all visible on your profile. Follow someone interesting and you get a window into their whole travel life — not just the photos they chose to post.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Stippl's travel community free?

Yes. Creating a profile, following other travellers, sharing trips and discovering recommendations is completely free in Stippl on web, iOS and Android.

### How do I find other travellers to follow on Stippl?

Search for friends by name or username, or discover new travellers through their published trips and shared places. Follow anyone whose recommendations you find interesting.

### Can I share my trip itinerary with the Stippl community?

Yes. Publish any trip itinerary to your Stippl profile and the community can browse it for inspiration. You control which trips are public and which stay private.

### Is my Stippl profile private?

Your profile is public by default so other travellers can discover your recommendations, but you control which trips and journal entries you share. Anything you don't publish stays visible only to you.

### Can I use Stippl as a travel social network?

Yes. Stippl works as a social network built specifically for travel — follow friends, share adventures, discover places through people you trust and build a record of your travel life in one place.
