## How does a travel budget planner work?

A travel budget planner helps you track what you spend on a trip, stay within your limits and settle shared costs with travel companions. Instead of guessing at the end of a holiday how much you spent — or ending up in complicated group expense calculations — a budget planner keeps everything recorded and clear from day one.

Stippl's travel budget planner lets you log expenses by category (accommodation, food, transport, activities), see a live breakdown of your spending and visualise where your money is going with charts. Set a total trip budget and track how much you have left at any point during your travels.

For group trips, the expense splitter handles the maths automatically. Add shared expenses, set who paid and who owes what, and Stippl calculates the simplest way to settle up — splitting costs evenly or unevenly between specific people. No more awkward spreadsheets or vague group chat calculations.

Stippl's budget planner supports over 100 currencies with automatic conversion, so multi-country trips with different currencies are no problem. All your spending lands in one place, converted and categorised, giving you a clear picture of your total trip cost regardless of where you spent it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Stippl's travel budget planner free?

Yes. The travel budget planner is completely free to use on web, iOS and Android. Track expenses, split costs and export data at no charge.

### How does the group expense splitter work?

Add shared expenses to your trip, mark who paid and Stippl calculates who owes what to whom. It works out the minimum number of transactions needed to settle all debts — fewer transfers, less confusion.

### Does the budget planner support multiple currencies?

Yes. Stippl automatically converts expenses across 100+ currencies. Log costs in the local currency for each destination and the app keeps your total in your home currency.

### Can I export my travel expenses?

Yes. Export your full trip expense data to CSV for personal finance records, employer reimbursements or your own review after the trip.

### Can I set a budget limit for my trip?

Yes. Set a total budget for your trip and Stippl tracks your running total against it, so you can see at a glance how much you have left to spend.
