## What is a travel itinerary planner?

A travel itinerary planner is a tool for organising all parts of a trip in one place — destinations, dates, accommodation, transport, activities and notes. Rather than tracking everything across emails, spreadsheets and multiple booking sites, an itinerary planner brings it together in a single structured timeline you can share and update.

Stippl's itinerary planner is built around how real trips work. Add multiple destinations in order, drag and drop stops to rearrange your route, and fill each day with activities, restaurants and accommodation found directly inside the app. Whether you're planning a weekend city break or a three-month backpacking trip, the planner scales to the complexity of your trip.

Planning with friends and family is built in — invite co-travellers to your trip, gather suggestions and collaborate on the final itinerary without the group chat chaos. Once everything is set, take the itinerary on the road with Stippl's mobile app for offline access.

A good holiday planner covers more than just sightseeing. Stippl connects your itinerary to transport booking (flights, trains, buses), accommodation platforms (Booking.com, Airbnb, Hostelworld) and activity discovery — so your trip plan and your bookings stay in sync. No more cross-referencing confirmation emails to remember what you booked.
